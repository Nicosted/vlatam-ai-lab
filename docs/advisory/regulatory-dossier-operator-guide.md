# Regulatory dossier operator guide

Collect the following from the client before regulatory research begins. Use placeholders only to record that a fact is unknown; never infer missing facts from filenames, branding, or commercial claims.

## Client and trade facts

- Commercial product name; manufacturer; Argentine exporter; Spanish/EU importer and responsible market-placement party.
- Requested operation, destination/target market, origin details, invoice/proforma, Incoterm/logistics assumptions, packaging, presentation, quantities, and photos.

## Product facts

- Exact intended use and user/application context.
- Formulation, every active ingredient, concentration/unit, and CAS/EC/equivalent identifier when supplied.
- Technical data sheet, current SDS/MSDS, product labels, catalog, and composition declaration.

## Claims and certificates

- Exact ecological, organic, biological, sustainable, or environmental claim text.
- Certificate, issuing body, scope, product/version, validity dates, and the claim it supports.
- Record every supplied item as `provided_unreviewed` until a reviewed repository artifact supports or rejects it.

## Jurisdiction questions

- Argentina export/customs and product/sector-authority requirements.
- HS/NCM evidence required for a classification hypothesis; do not supply a final classification as intake fact unless separately reviewed.
- Spain import/customs and importer obligations.
- EU product/chemical, labeling/safety, environmental-claim, and market-placement requirements.
- Logistics/documentation requirements and required trade, customs, product-safety, chemical, and claim-review professionals.

Research cannot begin when intended use, active ingredients/concentrations, SDS/MSDS, or importer identity is missing, or when composition conflicts remain. The operator must not enter legal conclusions, credentials, provider/model metadata, absolute paths, private raw document content, or downstream approval.
